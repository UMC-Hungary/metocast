[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), as recorded on Wikimedia Commons;
most were first published on Unsplash while it used CC0. They are cropped to 1920×1080 and
re-encoded. Replace any of them under Presentation → Presentation Settings → Welcome Screen.
